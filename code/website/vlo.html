How it works

Three steps, zero lab coat.

01

Clip it to the bar

The Movesense Flash snaps into a low-profile sleeve mount. Under 10 grams, no cables, pairs once over Bluetooth.

02

Lift like normal

The sensor's onboard IMU samples acceleration at 104 Hz. V-Lo integrates it into bar position and velocity in real time, rep by rep.

03

Autoregulate the set

When velocity loss crosses your threshold, V-Lo tells you to stop, drop weight, or push on — before the bar speed does it for you.

Training zones

Load by bar speed, not by feel.

V-Lo classifies every rep against published velocity-based training zones, calibrated per lift and per lifter over time.

Starting strength> 1.30 m/sbodyweightOvercoming inertia from a dead stop: jumps and throws
Speed-strength1.00 – 1.30 m/s20–40%Speed first, strength second: light loads, fast
Strength-speed0.75 – 1.00 m/s40–60%Moderately heavy loads, as fast as possible
Accelerative strength0.50 – 0.75 m/s65–80%Driving heavy loads as fast as possible
Absolute strength< 0.50 m/s80–100%Maximal force: heavy triples, doubles, singles

The five velocity zones from the VBT literature (Bryan Mann, via GymAware), judged on each set’s average bar speed and the same for squat, bench and deadlift. % of 1RM is typical, not a rule: the measured speed decides the zone.

      What format does the plan need to be in?

      Short answer: the format is forgiving. One exercise per line is the only hard rule. Everything else is optional and can be written in the order you like — the parser picks tokens out of the line and treats whatever is left as the exercise name.

      You writeIt reads as
      Week 3Starts a new week
      Day 2 — HeavyStarts a new day (also Monday, or any line ending in :)
      5x35 sets of 3 reps (5 x 3-5 and 3,3,2 work too)
      @140kgAbsolute load (315lb converts)
      @85%Percentage of your estimated 1RM
      @RPE8Rate of perceived exertion
      @31X1Tempo — see below
      // belt onA note (brackets work too)

      Tempo uses the standard four-character notation: eccentric · pause · concentric · pause, in seconds, where X means "as fast as you can". So 33X0 is three seconds down, three seconds held in the bottom, explosive up, no pause at the top. For the deadlift the order flips to concentric-first, because the pull is the first thing that happens.

      Word and Excel files are read the same way. A table with headers — Exercise, Sets, Reps, Load, Tempo, Notes, plus optional Week and Day columns — is read column by column. A table with no headers is read as a grid: labels along the top become days, labels down the side become weeks, and each cell is an exercise. Word headings and Excel sheet names become week and day labels too. Whatever is extracted lands in the box on the right, so you can correct it before it's saved.

      Anything the parser can't place is listed underneath the plan rather than silently dropped, so you can see exactly what it missed.
